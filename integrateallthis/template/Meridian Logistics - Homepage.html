About Meridian

Logistics expertise that keeps businesses moving.

Meridian Logistics is a global freight forwarding and supply chain partner, founded in 1998 on a single conviction — that complex cargo deserves one accountable team from origin to final mile, not a relay of handoffs between brokers and carriers.

We integrate air, ocean, road, warehousing and customs into one orchestrated operation, giving manufacturers, retailers and enterprises the reach of a global network with the responsiveness of a dedicated team — and the visibility to make decisions before problems become delays.
